# UI, UX & Motion

- Consistent spacing, typography and colors from the Design section of
  `docs/PROJECT.md`.
- Every action gives feedback: pressed state, loading, success, error.
- Every screen has empty, loading and error states.
- Motion is purposeful: 150–300ms, ease-out for entering, ease-in for
  leaving. Animate transitions, list changes, number changes and charts.
- Respect "reduce motion" settings. No animation that blocks the user.
- Large tap targets, readable contrast, works on small screens.
- Log non-obvious design and motion decisions in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
